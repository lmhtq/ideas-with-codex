# NEXUSKIN X-01 source notes

These sources informed the fictional product rationale. The deck treats them as
proof-of-principle research directions, not as evidence that the integrated suit
is currently manufacturable.

- HASEL electrohydraulic artificial muscles: Nature Communications 2024,
  "An untethered electrohydraulic crawling robot based on soft active joints."
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51568-3
- Non-invasive neural motor interface: Nature 2025, "A generic non-invasive
  neuromotor interface for human-computer interaction."
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09255-w
- Wireless, breathable electronic skin: Nature Electronics 2024,
  "A three-dimensional liquid diode for soft, integrated permeable electronics."
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-024-01189-x
- Wireless tactile feedback interface: Nature 2024, "A sensory haptic interface
  based on integrated tactile sensors and multimodal haptic feedback."
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08155-9
- Neuromorphic tactile closed-loop prosthesis basis: Nature Communications 2024,
  "A neuromorphic sensory processing system for neuroprostheses."
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-44723-3
- Auxetic impact structures: NIST / npj Computational Materials 2024,
  "Autonomous design algorithm to experimentally realize three-dimensionally
  isotropic auxetic metamaterials."
  https://www.nist.gov/publications/autonomous-design-algorithm-experimentally-realize-three-dimensionally-isotropic
- Shear-thickening soft armor direction: Springer 2026 review, "Shear thickening
  fluid-impregnated fabrics for impact protection."
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10443-026-10485-y
